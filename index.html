Navigation built for trucks, not cars.

The problem

Car apps put 20-tonne vehicles on roads that can't take them.

Truck drivers in India depend on navigation apps made for cars. They ignore height, weight and axle limits, fail without signal and make drivers juggle several tools.

⚠

Safety & compliance

Routes through low bridges, narrow roads and restricted zones lead to accidents, fines and bridge strikes.

🧾

Operational inefficiency

No integrated mileage, toll or expense tracking. Fragmented workflows and extra paperwork.

📵

Poor usability & connectivity

Cluttered interfaces, frequent crashes and no maps in remote areas.

The solution

One dependable co-pilot for the whole trip.

📐

Dimension-based routing

Filters paths by height, weight and axle configuration, set once in your vehicle profile.

📴

Offline functionality

Pre-downloaded maps and a local cache keep navigation steady with no signal.

💸

Automated logistics

Tolls, fuel consumption and mileage are logged automatically per trip.

🛡

Value proposition

Fewer accidents, no restricted-zone fines and a calmer driver.

🗣

Voice turn-by-turn

Spoken directions and early warnings for restrictions ahead.

⛽

Search along route

Fuel, parking, rest and weigh stations along your route.

📣

Driver feedback

Report wrong roads and missing bridges to improve the map.

🎵

TruckPlay

Calm, offline-ready driving music built into the app.

Objectives

  • Safety: minimise bridge strikes and impassable roads
  • Compliance: respect weight and time-based entry rules
  • Efficiency: automate expense logging
  • Accessibility: works regardless of internet

Why not Trucker Path / Google Maps?

  • Lack Indian road height and weight data
  • Crashes, wrong fuel-stop information
  • Paid features hidden behind a free-looking app
  • Navigation and expenses live in separate apps

Modular design

Five building blocks.

01

Authentication & Profile

Login and vehicle profiles with height, weight and axles.

02

Navigation & Routing

Mapbox-powered, truck-legal route engine.

03

POI & Search

Diesel stations, parking, weigh stations, rest areas.

04

Expense & Trip Tracker

Mileage, tolls, fuel and maintenance logs.

05

Offline Data Manager

Local caching and downloaded map regions.

Functional requirements

  • Voice turn-by-turn with offline caching
  • Full truck dimension and load profile
  • Real-time POI search along the route
  • Auto mileage and toll logging
  • Automatic offline switching
  • Driver feedback to report map errors

Technical architecture

  • Mobile: Flutter / React Native
  • Maps: Mapbox Navigation SDK
  • Backend: Node.js + Express
  • Data: MongoDB + Redis cache
  • Hardware: GPS + local storage

SDG alignment

  • SDG 9 – Industry & innovation
  • SDG 11 – Sustainable cities
